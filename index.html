How it works

  1. Photograph each garment against a plain wall — on a hanger or flat.
  2. Drop the photos in. Backgrounds come off on your own device.
  3. Tap a card to crop it square, straighten it, or correct the colour.
  4. File each one under a category and filter the grid by it.

Nothing is uploaded. The catalogue stays in this browser — use export to keep a backup or move it to another device.
